Headscale manuscript readiness assessment — 15 October 2026

The objective remains a software-engineering paper suitable for CCF B or a
second-quartile journal. The current output is a research working draft.
Neither manuscript length, dataset size nor passing tests proves that quality.
Venue classifications do not grade an individual article.

Verified evidence
- Git analysis is pinned to the official v0.29.5 release commit. The v0.28.0
  baseline and original coursework remain preserved.
- Metadata, submitted reviews and lifecycle timelines cover all 2,166 census PRs.
- All 1,318 merged human-account PRs have complete file lists matching metadata.
- Author, reviewer and integrator activity are compared on matched PR populations.
  Role differences also occur in Go-touched and exclusively-Go-file subsets.
- Reopened PRs are reconstructed into initial episodes. Final state, episode
  outcomes and historical pending queues are kept distinct.
- Exploratory models include role/experience strata, 30/90/180-day exposures,
  omitted-backlog checks and elapsed-time-varying concentration coefficients.
  Constant-effect intervals include one. The late association involves a small,
  selected risk set and does not establish a causal maintenance benefit.
- Raw pages, numerical sources, fit scripts, dependency lock and figure inputs
  have provenance checks. The 41-test suite covers known hazards, temporal risk
  boundaries, review-credit conservation, adjudication and exact commit linkage.
- All 100 sampled issues have neutral offline review packets in each reviewer's
  existing order. Full SHA matching links 10 issues to 12 merged PRs; only 48 of
  243 distinct referenced commits are locally readable. These are evidence
  pointers and explicit coverage limits, not verified repairs or human labels.
- Missing reviewer identities are now bounded on the fixed 1,259 known-reviewed
  merged PRs. There are 177 eligible unattributed records on 74 PRs in four
  quarters. Integrator HHI exceeds the conservative reviewer upper bound in
  each affected quarter. Conditional known-only and all-distinct-account
  scenarios reverse reviewer/author ordering in 2025 Q2 and Q3. These are
  collected-record sensitivity results, not actual identity imputations,
  confidence intervals or a new bus-factor method. Deleted and informal
  reviews and known-account aliases remain outside their coverage.
- Two source-available final-closure commits with exact merged-PR matches now
  have executed component checks against their official first parents. Three
  regression oracles fail before and pass after the repairs; two normal-case
  controls pass on both sides. Source, fixture and dependency hashes are checked,
  as are the actual semantic failure messages. An additional cached execution
  reproduced the outcomes. These are two cases, not independent replications or
  full replays of the original DNS/client and Kubernetes deployments. They do
  not supply the deferred human labels or a population repair prevalence.

Novelty assessment
Ownership, integration, response latency, Stale bot, issue misclassification and
OSS-health assessment already have directly related research. HHI, a Linux port
and more commits do not establish a new contribution. The candidate explanation
concerns how maintenance roles and component-responsibility boundaries affect
interpretation of maintenance outcomes in an upstream-dependent implementation.
Role observations are available; that explanatory mechanism is not validated.

The scoped search also verifies a peer-reviewed Headscale VPN-performance study
and a direct review-aware bus-factor precedent. They limit first-study and
role-separation novelty claims, but do not establish duplication of this exact
maintenance-process question. The search is not exhaustive. A conditional B-
conference assessment, with ESEM as the preferred scope fit and ICSME as a
maintenance-focused alternative, is recorded in submission/venue_assessment.txt.
The present draft has not yet established the validated additional knowledge
needed for either research main track.

Remaining requirements
1. Conduct actual human responsibility/outcome coding of the fixed sample.
   The author has deferred this stage. Two-reviewer and declared single-reviewer
   designs remain available; neither has been completed. Assistant suggestions,
   closure timestamps and missing commit links cannot supply repair truth.
2. Trace responsibility evidence to explicit maintenance decisions and treatment.
   Examine unclear and contradictory cases. Relate the result to the closest
   prior work; if only known measurement pitfalls recur, strengthen comparison
   or triangulation rather than claim an established novel mechanism.
3. Interpret robustness within its limits. Episode coalescing and quarter
   influence checks do not remove selection, confounding or limited clustering,
   and cannot replace semantic validation.
4. Complete author review, settle corresponding authorship and any additional
   authors, supply funding/competing-interest declarations, and finalize the
   selected venue's format, data-access statement and AI disclosure.
