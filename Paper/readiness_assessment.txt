- Metadata, submitted reviews and lifecycle timelines cover all 2,166 census PRs.
- All 1,318 merged human-account PRs have complete file lists matching metadata.

  Role differences also occur in Go-touched and exclusively-Go-file subsets.

  outcomes and historical pending queues are kept distinct.

  Constant-effect intervals include one. The late association involves a small,

  243 distinct referenced commits are locally readable. These are evidence

  full replays of the original DNS/client and Kubernetes deployments. They do

Novelty assessment
Ownership, integration, response latency, Stale bot, issue misclassification and
OSS-health assessment already have directly related research. HHI, a Linux port
and more commits do not establish a new contribution. The candidate explanation
concerns how maintenance roles and component-responsibility boundaries affect
interpretation of maintenance outcomes in an upstream-dependent implementation.
Role observations are available; that explanatory mechanism is not validated.

The scoped search also verifies a peer-reviewed Headscale VPN-performance study
and a direct review-aware bus-factor precedent. They limit first-study and
role-separation novelty claims, but do not establish duplication of this exact
maintenance-process question. The search is not exhaustive. A conditional B-
conference assessment, with ESEM as the preferred scope fit and ICSME as a
maintenance-focused alternative, is recorded in submission/venue_assessment.txt.
The present draft has not yet established the validated additional knowledge
needed for either research main track.

  establish separate groups, and self-merging does not establish no review.

  all 56 late merges. This weakens any robust-benefit interpretation of the
  earlier precise late estimate; it does not prove a difference between models.
- The DevSkill institutional full text is now inspected. Its Headscale figure
  and explicit identity-splitting caveat narrow the contribution claim; this
