IST fit and current risk
The 2026 editors' discussion emphasizes scope, novelty/impact and methodological
quality, and identifies insufficient validation and premature work as rejection
risks. My assessment of this manuscript is that another repository showing
concentrated roles and non-repair closures is insufficient by itself. A specific
process explanation and its boundaries need validation, or a transparent
comparative design must establish the finding's wider applicability.

Boundaries of the candidate contribution
Headscale-specific search now verifies a 2024 VPN benchmark study and a 2025
repository-mining thesis whose full text is now inspected. The manuscript cannot claim the first academic
study of Headscale. Bus Factor In Practice (ICSE-SEIP 2022) is also a direct
precedent for considering low-authorship reviewers. The detailed overlap and
search-access limits are in literature_overlap_assessment.txt.
1. Review-aware ownership already distinguishes authoring and reviewing.
   Matching PR populations, fractional review credit and artifact-scope
   sensitivity apply this distinction; they do not introduce it.
2. Large-scale CODEOWNERS research already examines review dynamics. One
   static ownership file cannot establish policy effects. Lulla et al.'s
   August 2026 version is cited as an author preprint.
3. Issue misclassification and bot closure have direct precedents. Missing
   commit links cannot establish absence of repair.
4. Linåker et al.'s recent OSS-health study combines practitioner interviews,
   framework comparison and an industrial application, and considers governance
   concentration and context. Role separation alone is not a new health framework.
   The narrower proposed explanation requires tracing component-responsibility
   evidence through a maintenance decision to the observed treatment.

Current decision
The data and computational checks support substantive review, but do not prove
the proposed mechanism or sufficient novelty. Human coding is deferred at the
author's request. Continue manuscript preparation while retaining semantic
validation, corresponding authorship and final declarations as unfinished work.

The 15 October joint-account census and calendar-control audit sharpen these
limits. Self-merging usually coexists with recorded other-account review;
separate role HHIs do not establish separate teams. The late merge association
becomes imprecise under within-creation-cohort risk sets. These checks provide
more defensible case interpretation, but neither establishes a new general
method, causal benefit or the proposed responsibility-routing mechanism.

Conference assessment after the scoped literature search
CCF B remains a defensible research target, conditional on establishing an
additional validated contribution. The present working draft does not yet
establish readiness for a B-conference research full paper. This is an assessment
of the evidence and contribution, not an acceptance-probability estimate.

The current official CCF software-engineering directory places both ESEM and
ICSME in category B. Its catalog guidance applies to Full/Regular papers; a
short paper, demo or accompanying workshop cannot be assumed to meet this goal.
The official directory's complete table was returned through indexed search;
direct browser retrieval of the CCF page failed during this check.

ESEM's technical main track is the preferred fit for the present emphasis on
empirical measurement and validity. Its 2026 call explicitly includes case
studies, replication and negative results, while requiring fully developed
research, rigorous methods and clear value beyond existing knowledge. Thus,
neither a single case nor a concentration estimate whose interval includes one
automatically rules out a contribution. Artifact completeness alone does not
establish that value.
Source:
https://conf.researchr.org/track/eseiw-2026/eseiw-2026-esem---technical-track

ICSME's research track is a second fit if the work establishes a substantive
maintenance-process explanation. Its criteria explicitly assess originality,
importance, soundness, evaluation, related-work comparison and replicability.

These 2026 editions have already concluded. Their calls establish recent scope
and standards; no future-cycle deadline or submission availability is assumed.

The verified Headscale VPN-performance paper does not answer our maintenance
questions. However, Bus Factor In Practice directly precedes review-aware
interpretation, and has practitioner ground truth. Finding low-authorship
reviewers in another repository is insufficient as the main novelty claim.

The strongest candidate question is how component-responsibility boundaries and
different maintenance roles alter conclusions drawn from repository indicators.
To establish its value, the study needs:
1. Actual, declared human validation of responsibility and treatment outcomes.
   This stage remains deferred; prepared packets and extracted events are not
   completed validation.
2. Evidence connecting the affected component to an explicit maintenance
   decision and observed treatment, with ambiguous and contradictory cases.
   Quantify how validated interpretation changes a clearly defined conventional
   measurement or substantive conclusion; do not claim causal policy effects.
3. A contribution beyond known pitfalls, supported either by a deeply validated,
   bounded case explanation or by purposeful comparative/triangulating evidence.
   If the case only reproduces established observations, additional comparable
   cases may help test applicability, but more repositories cannot create
   novelty by themselves.

Proceed with the B-conference objective while treating contribution validation
as the priority. More commits, charts, model variants or manuscript pages alone
would not resolve the current gap.
