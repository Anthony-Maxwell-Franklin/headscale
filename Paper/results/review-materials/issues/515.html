Report body

First of all, thank you for this great project. Everything is working as good as it could and most of the issues seem to be stemming from our attempt to override the control plane for Tailscale clients, and not from behavioral differences of the control plane itself.

So far no matter what I have done, I wasn't able to get the iOS Tailscale client to respect the profile from `/apple/ios` and communicate with my control server. And `tailscale-ios` isn't open-sourced unlike `tailscale-android` preventing us from being unable to write a quick patch? Is this known or expected?
